About SpineSync

A modern spine and physiotherapy clinic built around one idea: care that fits your life.

SpineSync was founded in 2014 by a small team of chiropractors and physiotherapists who believed recovery should be personal, evidence-based and genuinely supportive. Over twelve years we've grown into a multidisciplinary clinic on Harley Street — but our principle hasn't changed.

We take time to understand each patient's history, goals and day-to-day life, then build a plan around them. Our integrated digital portal lets you track progress, follow your exercise programme and stay connected with your care team between sessions.

Patient-first
Every plan is built around you, not just your diagnosis.
Evidence-based
Treatments grounded in the latest clinical research.
Multidisciplinary
A connected team across every stage of recovery.

Before you arrive

When you book, we create your patient portal account and send you a new-patient intake form. Completing it online saves about ten minutes at the desk, and your practitioner reads it before you walk in.

Arrive five minutes early so we can check you in without eating into your session.
Wear comfortable clothing you can move in. We have gowns and shorts if you come straight from work.
Open the patient portal
Children and under-18s

A parent or guardian must book the appointment, give consent to treatment, and attend the first visit. Bring proof of your relationship if the surname differs.

Guardians can see and manage a child's appointments, forms and balances from a single portal login, and records stay linked until the young person turns 18 — at which point access is reviewed with them.

Insurance & Payment

Insurance & Payment

Clear pricing, recognised by every major UK insurer, and no surprises on your invoice.

Paying for yourself

Every treatment has a published price — you'll know the cost before you book, and we'll tell you how many sessions we expect at your assessment. Payment is taken after each visit.

Using private insurance

Bring your membership number and pre-authorisation code to your first visit and we'll invoice your insurer directly wherever your policy allows.

Packages and memberships

Prepaid blocks of six or twelve sessions, and monthly maintenance memberships, are available on request at reception. Unused visits and expiry dates are always visible in your portal.

The small print that matters

Where an insurer declines, delays or part-pays a claim, the balance remains payable by you. We'll always tell you before that happens rather than after.

Deposits apply to new-patient assessments and are offset against your first invoice. Late cancellations may incur a fee — see our .
