About SpineSync

A modern spine and physiotherapy clinic built around one idea: care that fits your life.

SpineSync was founded in 2014 by a small team of chiropractors and physiotherapists who believed recovery should be personal, evidence-based and genuinely supportive. Over twelve years we've grown into a multidisciplinary clinic on Harley Street — but our principle hasn't changed.

We take time to understand each patient's history, goals and day-to-day life, then build a plan around them. Our integrated digital portal lets you track progress, follow your exercise programme and stay connected with your care team between sessions.

Patient-first
Every plan is built around you, not just your diagnosis.
Evidence-based
Treatments grounded in the latest clinical research.
Multidisciplinary
A connected team across every stage of recovery.
